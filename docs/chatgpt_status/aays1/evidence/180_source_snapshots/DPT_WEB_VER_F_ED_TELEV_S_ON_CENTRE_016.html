The Home of Expression

Television Centre, historic homes of the BBC, set the foundation for the future. Now, it charts a new course as West London’s cultural core. A place for individuals everywhere to come together. To live, work, meet and explore. To live out who they really are among the homes, offices, dining and entertainment destinations that call White City their own.

This is the home of expression.
