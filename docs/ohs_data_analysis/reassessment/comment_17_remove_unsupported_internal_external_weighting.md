# Comment 17 — Remove the unsupported 0.10/0.90 internal–external weighting

## Reviewer comment
`Neden internal 0.1 external ise 0.9 ile çarpıldı.`

## Reassessment decision
The 0.10/0.90 split cannot be defended from the archived evidence and will be removed from the manuscript and application description. The available package contains no empirical estimation, expert-elicitation protocol, legal or regulatory rule, optimization criterion, sensitivity analysis, calibration experiment, validation study, or cited external source that establishes that the internal component should contribute 10% and the external component 90%.

The values appear to have been imposed as fixed contribution caps rather than estimated weights. This is methodologically important because the manuscript subsequently interprets 6.25% as an internal contribution out of 10%, 58.18% as an external contribution out of 90%, and 64.43% as an overall risk percentage. Those quantities are therefore consequences of the arbitrary allocation and must not be presented as empirically validated risk percentages.

## Scientific rationale
A weighted composite is defensible only when all of the following are documented:
1. the two components measure a coherent common construct;
2. their scales and directions are compatible or are transformed using a prespecified, validated procedure;
3. the weights are estimated or elicited using a reproducible method;
4. the composite is validated against an external criterion or decision outcome;
5. uncertainty and sensitivity to the selected weights are reported.

None of these conditions is currently satisfied. Moreover, the so-called internal and external components are derived from heterogeneous variables and different aggregation procedures. Adding them after imposing 10% and 90% caps creates an appearance of comparability and precision that the evidence does not support.

## Locked corrections
- Delete every equation or narrative statement that multiplies the internal component by 0.10 and the external component by 0.90.
- Delete the claims that internal factors account for approximately 10% and external factors approximately 90% of total occupational risk.
- Delete the illustrated `maximum contribution = 10%`, `maximum = 90%`, and `overall risk percentage = 64.43%` statements.
- Remove any gauge, progress bar, table, figure, or interface field that displays the sum as a unified validated risk percentage.
- Do not substitute equal weights, literature-derived weights, or expert weights unless a new reproducible study explicitly establishes them.
- Report any retained internal and external quantities separately, with distinct names, equations, scales, ranges, and interpretations.
- Do not compare their magnitudes directly unless they are first placed on a validated common scale.

## Approved replacement wording
`The application reports the retained person-related and work-environment-related components separately. No overall weighted score was calculated because the available study materials did not provide an empirical or expert-elicitation basis for assigning relative weights to the two components, and their numerical scales were not shown to be directly commensurable.`

## Reporting boundary
The separately reported component values are descriptive decision-support scores. They are not probabilities, attributable fractions, exposure rates, causal contributions, or percentages of an individual's total occupational risk.
