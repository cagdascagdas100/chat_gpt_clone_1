## Latest decision — Comment 17
The fixed 0.10/0.90 internal–external weighting will be removed. The archived project package provides no empirical estimation, expert-elicitation protocol, legal rule, optimization criterion, sensitivity analysis, validation study, or cited source supporting a 10% contribution for the internal component and a 90% contribution for the external component.

## Consequences for the manuscript
- Delete the equations and statements that multiply the internal component by 0.10 and the external component by 0.90.
- Delete the claim that internal factors account for approximately 10% and external factors approximately 90% of total occupational risk.
- Delete the example outputs `internal risk percentage = 6.25%`, `external risk percentage = 58.18%`, and `overall risk percentage = 64.43%` when they are produced by these arbitrary caps.
- Remove any publication-facing or application-facing unified percentage based on this sum.
- Do not replace the weights with equal or literature-derived values without a new reproducible derivation and validation.

## Approved replacement
`The application reports the retained person-related and work-environment-related components separately. No overall weighted score was calculated because the available study materials did not provide an empirical or expert-elicitation basis for assigning relative weights to the two components, and their numerical scales were not shown to be directly commensurable.`

## Interpretation boundary
Separately reported component values are descriptive decision-support scores. They are not probabilities, attributable fractions, exposure rates, causal contributions, or percentages of an individual's total occupational risk. Their magnitudes must not be compared directly unless a validated common scale is established.
