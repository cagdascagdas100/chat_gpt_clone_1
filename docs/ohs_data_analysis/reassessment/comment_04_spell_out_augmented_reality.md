# Reassessment — Comment 4: spell out `augmented reality` and preserve bibliographic titles

## Reviewer comment
`AR kısaltma vermeyelim, direkt augmented reality şeklinde yazalım.`

## Final editorial decision
All narrative uses of the abbreviation `AR` will be replaced with the full term `augmented reality`. Where the manuscript currently uses paired shorthand such as `AR/VR` or `VR/AR`, the prose will use `augmented reality and virtual reality` in full.

The abbreviation `AR` will be retained only where it forms part of the exact published title of a cited work in the reference list. Bibliographic titles must not be silently rewritten.

## Manuscript audit
The visible manuscript body contains the following relevant uses:
- the Introduction already uses `augmented reality` and `virtual reality` in full and requires no abbreviation;
- the literature-review paragraph describing Aksu and Ofluoğlu [18] uses `AR` twice;
- the following paragraph uses `AR/VR`, `VR/AR`, and related compounds;
- a later synthesis sentence uses `AR/VR or 3D-based systems`;
- reference [18] contains `AR` in the official article title.

## Recommended replacements
### Aksu and Ofluoğlu paragraph
Replace abbreviation-based wording with:

`Aksu and Ofluoğlu [18] proposed a mobile platform that combines building information modeling with augmented reality to support fall-risk prevention by displaying relevant precautions. In that system, augmented reality was used to present BIM-linked safety information within the physical work environment.`

This revision removes the abbreviation and also corrects the incomplete sentence structure in the current paragraph.

### Education paragraph
Replace:
`In another application of AR/VR ... using AR/VR-based mobile applications ... between VR/AR and traditional groups`

with:
`In another application of augmented reality and virtual reality technologies, Le et al. [25] developed mobile learning modules for experiential construction-safety education. The system was evaluated through usability testing and a comparison between augmented-reality/virtual-reality-assisted instruction and traditional instruction.`

The final manuscript may further simplify the slash construction to `instruction supported by augmented reality and virtual reality` if required by the journal style.

### Synthesis sentence
Replace:
`AR/VR or 3D-based systems`

with:
`augmented-reality-, virtual-reality-, or three-dimensional systems`

## Scope boundary
These technologies are discussed only as examples from prior literature. They must not be presented as implemented components of OSH-RA. The verified OSH-RA architecture does not document runtime augmented-reality or virtual-reality functionality.

## Reference-list rule
Reference [18] should preserve its exact published title:
`A Proposal of a BIM and AR Integrated Application Against Fall Risks in Construction Projects.`

Changing `AR` inside the article title would reduce bibliographic accuracy. The reviewer request is therefore applied to manuscript prose, headings, captions, tables, and notes, but not to exact source titles.
