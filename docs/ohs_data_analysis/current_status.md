## Latest decision — Comment 32
The note `Bundan sonrası yine karmaşıklıktı` is anchored to `dimensions of the risks` in the penultimate Conclusion paragraph. The defensible scope is the remainder of that paragraph and the final cross-sector paragraph. Both will be replaced by one concise, evidence-bounded closing paragraph.

## Required conclusion simplification
- Remove unverified claims concerning a deployed combination of ML, fuzzy logic, and LLM-driven reasoning.
- Remove auditory/visual-input, autonomous-update, live-data-stream, and continuous-adaptation claims from the present-system description.
- Remove the unsupported assertion that cost-related display fields constitute a validated economic-risk model.
- Remove claims of successful or seamless transfer to manufacturing, logistics, healthcare, or other sectors.
- Retain only the verified architecture: structured inputs, offline-trained classifiers, deterministic aggregation/ranking rules, and mobile presentation of decision-support outputs.

## Approved replacement ending
`In summary, OSH-RA integrates structured worker and worksite inputs, offline-trained classifiers, deterministic risk-aggregation and ranking rules, and a mobile interface that presents risk summaries, prioritized equipment–fault scenarios, recommended precautions, legal-information content, and cost-related outputs. The current study reports internal analytical evaluation and an illustrative application workflow only. It does not establish real-time monitoring, multimodal inference, autonomous updating, external validity, cross-sector transferability, usability, or field effectiveness. Independent prospective evaluation in construction settings is therefore required before operational deployment or extension to other sectors.`

## Valid limitations retained
- Internal validation without a documented independent external cohort.
- Country- and dataset-specific transportability constraints.
- Need for prospectively available, leakage-safe predictors.
- Unresolved aggregation-weight and regional-decay parameter choices.
- Missing usability, fairness, privacy, field-effectiveness, and economic-impact evaluation.
