## Practical-use content finalized
- OSH-RA is described as a mobile occupational-safety decision-support application, not as a standalone `model`.
- Workers provide structured personal, task, work-area, machine, and observed machine-condition information.
- Occupational-safety professionals, competent persons, and site supervisors review the results and decide whether inspection, maintenance, repair, isolation, task suspension, or another control is required.
- The application is used as a repeated point-in-time assessment when the worker, shift, work area, machine, or observed condition changes; it is not continuous real-time monitoring.
- The verified outputs are an overall risk summary, body-region distribution, prioritized machine–fault scenarios, recommended precautions, consequences of no controls, legal-information content, and cost-related outputs.
- The application supplements rather than replaces statutory risk assessment, competent-person judgment, engineering controls, maintenance procedures, and emergency arrangements.

## Evidence and safeguard boundaries
- Current evidence supports internal analytical evaluation and documentation of application functionality only.
- No claim will be made for external validity, usability, user acceptance, accident reduction, economic benefit, or field effectiveness.
- Personal-risk outputs must not be used for blame, discriminatory job allocation, or denial of work.
- Legal-information output is not individualized legal advice.
- Cost information must not be used to postpone or trade away mandatory safety controls.
