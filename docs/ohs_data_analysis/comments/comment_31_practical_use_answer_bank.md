## Short answer bank for Section 5.3

### What is OSH-RA?
OSH-RA is a mobile occupational-safety decision-support application. It combines structured worker and worksite inputs, offline-trained classifiers, deterministic risk aggregation and ranking rules, and screen-based presentation of risk-management outputs.

### Who is expected to use it?
Workers provide observable personal, task, work-area, machine, and machine-condition information. Occupational-safety professionals, competent persons, and site supervisors review the outputs and determine whether inspection, maintenance, repair, isolation, task suspension, or another control is required. Management may review aggregated control and cost information for planning purposes.

### When is it intended to be used?
The assessment is intended before or during task preparation and whenever the worker, shift, work area, machine, or observed machine condition changes. It is a repeated point-in-time assessment, not continuous real-time monitoring.

### What information is entered?
The verified workflow uses structured personal and contextual variables, followed by hierarchical selection of sector, category, subcategory, work section, machine, and observed machine fault or condition.

### What does the application display?
The application displays an overall risk summary, body-region-specific risk distribution, and prioritized machine–fault scenarios. Detailed screens provide visual results, recommended precautions, consequences of not implementing controls, legal-information content, and cost-related outputs.

### What decisions can the outputs support?
The outputs can help users prioritize which machine or fault should be reviewed first and which preventive action should be considered. Final control decisions remain the responsibility of competent personnel and must follow statutory requirements, site procedures, and engineering judgment.

### What does the application not replace?
It does not replace the legally required risk assessment, competent-person inspection, engineering judgment, maintenance procedures, emergency arrangements, or regulatory duties. The legal-information module is not individualized legal advice.

### What is the current evidence level?
The current evidence supports internal model evaluation only. It does not establish application-level superiority, external validity, usability, user acceptance, accident reduction, economic benefit, or field effectiveness.

### What safeguards are required?
Personal-risk outputs must not be used to blame workers, assign work discriminatorily, deny employment, or postpone mandatory controls. Cost information must not be interpreted as permission to trade required safety measures against financial savings.

## Recommended compact manuscript wording
`The OSH-RA mobile decision-support application is intended for repeated point-in-time use when a worker, shift, work area, machine, or observed machine condition changes. Workers enter structured personal, task, work-area, machine, and machine-condition information; occupational-safety professionals, competent persons, and site supervisors may then review the resulting risk summary, body-region distribution, prioritized machine–fault scenarios, recommended precautions, legal-information content, and cost-related outputs. These outputs are intended to support prioritization of inspection, maintenance, repair, isolation, or other controls, but they do not replace statutory risk assessment, competent-person judgment, engineering controls, or emergency procedures. The present study documents internal analytical performance and application functionality; it does not establish external validity, usability, accident-reduction effectiveness, or economic benefit.`
