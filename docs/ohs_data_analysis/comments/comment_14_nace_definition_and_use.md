# Comment 14 — Define NACE and clarify its use

## Reviewer comment
`Buraya bir yere nace’nin ne demek olduğu yazılmalı ayrıca açılımı da verilmeli.`

## Selected manuscript text
`NACE 41: Construction of Buildings, NACE 42: Civil Engineering, and NACE 43: Specialized Construction Activities (Figure 3).`

## Original sentence
`To define the exposure baseline, data from Social Security Institution (SSI) between 2019 to 2023 at three code levels were linked to historical accident records, which enabled automatic assignment of accidents to NACE 41: Construction of Buildings, NACE 42: Civil Engineering, and NACE 43: Specialized Construction Activities (Figure 3).`

## Verification and terminology decision
NACE is the European classification used to classify economic activities. The official English designation is `Statistical Classification of Economic Activities in the European Community`. Because the study data cover 2019–2023, the applicable version is NACE Revision 2 (NACE Rev. 2), not NACE Rev. 2.1. Eurostat states that NACE Rev. 2 applies to European statistics for 2008–2024, whereas NACE Rev. 2.1 is used from 2025 onward.

Codes 41, 42, and 43 are not three different `code levels`. They are three two-digit divisions at the same hierarchical level within NACE Section F (Construction):
- Division 41 — Construction of Buildings
- Division 42 — Civil Engineering
- Division 43 — Specialised Construction Activities

The official division label uses the spelling `Specialised`. Although the manuscript otherwise follows American English, the official classification label should be reproduced verbatim.

## Preferred replacement text
`To define the sectoral scope of the exposure baseline, administrative records from the Turkish Social Security Institution (SSI) for 2019–2023 were linked to the historical accident records using Revision 2 of the Statistical Classification of Economic Activities in the European Community (NACE Rev. 2). The analysis covered the three two-digit divisions in NACE Section F (Construction): Division 41 (Construction of Buildings), Division 42 (Civil Engineering), and Division 43 (Specialised Construction Activities) (Figure 3).`

## Why this revision is stronger
- Defines NACE at first use and gives its full official English name.
- Reports the version appropriate to the 2019–2023 observation period.
- Corrects `between 2019 to 2023` to `for 2019–2023`.
- Replaces the inaccurate phrase `at three code levels` with `three two-digit divisions`.
- Clarifies that NACE determines sectoral economic-activity scope rather than injury severity, machine type, or accident mechanism.
- Removes the unsupported phrase `enabled automatic assignment` unless the automated mapping procedure is explicitly documented elsewhere.
- Uses the full institutional name `Turkish Social Security Institution` at first mention.

## Citation decision
Add one authoritative source at the first definition of NACE. The preferred source is the official Eurostat NACE overview or the legal instrument establishing NACE Rev. 2. The citation should support the classification name, hierarchy, and applicable version; it should not be used to support claims about the study's own linkage procedure.

Official source consulted:
- Eurostat, `NACE — Overview`: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/nace/overview

The final manuscript reference number should be assigned only after the bibliography is renumbered.

## Red-highlight treatment for the final workbook
Because the original sentence contains several substantive and grammatical errors, the full two-sentence replacement should be shown. Only the inserted or replaced wording should be red in the complete revised paragraph.

## Quality re-audit and cross-comment consistency
- Comment 3 reserved `exposure` for an explicitly defined exposure construct. Therefore, this paragraph uses `sectoral scope of the exposure baseline` rather than implying that NACE itself measures worker exposure.
- The later statement that machine headings were `mapped to the NACE hierarchy` requires separate verification. NACE classifies economic activities, not equipment types. Unless a documented crosswalk exists, that wording should be revised in Comment 15 or during the final consistency audit.
- Figure 3, its caption, all table labels, and the data-preprocessing description must use `NACE Rev. 2`, `Section F`, and `Divisions 41–43` consistently.
- NACE Rev. 2.1 must not be substituted merely because it is the current version; the manuscript must report the classification version used for the 2019–2023 data.
