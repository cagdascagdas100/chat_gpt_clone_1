# Comment 22 — Separate descriptions for Figures 16, 17, and 18

## Reviewer comment
`Böyle tek bir cümlede değil, her bir figürün application’ın hangi kısmını gösterdiğini ayrı ayrı cümlelerde söyleyelim.`

## Selected manuscript sentence
`Figure 16, Figure 17, and Figure 18 illustrate the visual output screens of the OSH-RA mobile application, highlighting its interface and functionality, and offering detailed risk assessments, precautionary measures, legal rights, and cost analysis related to various machinery and work scenarios.`

## Editorial assessment
The original sentence is too broad and assigns several different application functions collectively to three figures. It does not tell the reader which screen appears in which figure, and the phrase `interface and functionality` is generic rather than descriptive. Each figure should therefore be introduced in a separate sentence using only the functions visible in that figure.

## Verified figure content
- Figure 16 contains the work-section selection screen, machine-selection/detail screen, and the overall risk-summary dashboard.
- Figure 17 contains the precaution-recommendation screen, legal-information screen, and body-region-specific risk visualization.
- Figure 18 contains the `What If No Precautions` consequence narratives and cost-analysis screens, including preventive-cost inputs and displayed payment/risk-cost fields.

## Preferred replacement paragraph
`Figure 16 presents the application workflow for selecting a work section and machine and shows the resulting overall risk classification and percentage. Figure 17 presents the detailed output modules for recommended precautions, relevant legal provisions, and body-region-specific risk values. Figure 18 presents the projected consequences of not implementing the recommended precautions and the cost-analysis interface, including preventive-cost inputs and the payment- and risk-cost fields displayed by the application.`

This version answers the reviewer directly, preserves the order of the figures, and avoids attributing the same functions to all three images.

## Preferred revised captions
- `Figure 16. OSH-RA mobile application: work-section and machine-selection workflow and overall risk-summary screen.`
- `Figure 17. OSH-RA mobile application: precaution recommendations, legal-information module, and body-region risk visualization.`
- `Figure 18. OSH-RA mobile application: unmitigated-consequence narratives and cost-analysis input/output screens.`

## Terminology safeguards
- Use `body-region-specific risk values`, not the vague expression `visual results`.
- Use `legal-information module` or `relevant legal provisions`; do not imply that the application provides individualized legal advice.
- In Figure 18, describe the visible fields neutrally. Terms such as `indemnity cost`, `payment`, and `risk cost` must remain subject to the variable-definition and unit-verification requirements established in Comments 8, 9, and 21.
- Do not call the displayed `overall risk percentage` validated or accurate unless the normalization and 0.10/0.90 aggregation rule are finalized under Comments 17 and 20.
- Avoid `various machinery and work scenarios` because Figure 16 shows one illustrative pipeline-equipment workflow rather than a representative sample of all application configurations.

## Cross-comment consistency
- Comment 20 governs interpretation of the displayed overall percentage and the distinction between component and composite scores.
- Comment 21 governs payment-day, cost, and CVaR terminology.
- Comment 23 may add further application screens, but the descriptions of Figures 16–18 remain valid and should not be merged back into one omnibus sentence.

## Reviewer-response draft
`Revised. The three application figures are now described in separate sentences. Figure 16 is identified as the work-section and machine-selection workflow and overall risk-summary screen; Figure 17 is identified as the precautions, legal-information, and body-region visualization module; and Figure 18 is identified as the unmitigated-consequence and cost-analysis module. The figure captions were revised accordingly.`
