# Comment 23 — Additional mobile-application figures

## Reviewer comment
`Mobil application ile ilgili daha çok figür koysak daha güzel olmaz mı, işte uygulama açılıncaki ilk sayfası modüller vs. örnek sayfalar gibisinden.`

## Selected context
The comment is attached to the Figure 18 caption, but it concerns the completeness of the application presentation across Figures 16–18.

## Evidence-based assessment of the current figures
The current main-text figures already document most result-facing modules:
- Figure 16 shows work-section selection, machine selection, and the overall risk-summary screen with links to the detailed modules.
- Figure 17 shows precaution recommendations, the legal-information module, and body-region-specific risk values.
- Figure 18 shows the unmitigated-hazard consequences and cost-analysis screens.

Therefore, adding several more stand-alone screenshots of the same modules would be redundant and would reduce figure legibility. The principal documentation gap is the beginning of the workflow: the landing/home screen and the entry of the personal-risk variables before the work-environment and machine-selection steps.

## Final editorial decision
Do not add an open-ended series of screenshots to the Results section. Expand the application documentation with one compact, panel-labeled workflow figure that shows the minimum end-to-end sequence. The preferred solution is to revise Figure 16 rather than create several new main-text figure numbers.

## Minimum screen set for the revised Figure 16
Use a two-row, multi-panel layout with no more than six panels:
- **(a) Landing/home screen:** application entry point and principal module structure.
- **(b) Personal-risk input screen:** anonymized entry of the individual variables used by the internal component.
- **(c) Work-area/activity selection:** selection of the relevant work context.
- **(d) Machine and malfunction selection:** selection of the equipment and the documented fault or hazard condition.
- **(e) Overall risk-summary screen:** displayed risk category and percentage, subject to the aggregation corrections in Comments 17 and 20.
- **(f) Detailed-output navigation:** links to precautions, legal information, body-region results, unmitigated-hazard consequences, and cost analysis.

Panels (c)–(f) are already represented in the current Figure 16. Only panels (a) and (b) require additional source screenshots. A second copy of the module tiles alone should not be added because they are already visible in the current overall risk-summary screen.

## Preferred caption
`Figure 16. OSH-RA mobile-application workflow: (a) landing page and module structure, (b) anonymized personal-risk input, (c) work-area and activity selection, (d) machine and malfunction selection, (e) overall risk summary, and (f) navigation to the detailed output modules.`

## Preferred manuscript text
`Figure 16 summarizes the end-to-end application workflow. The sequence begins with the landing page and entry of the anonymized personal-risk variables, continues through work-area, machine, and malfunction selection, and concludes with the overall risk-summary screen and navigation to the detailed precaution, legal-information, body-region, unmitigated-hazard, and cost-analysis modules. Figures 17 and 18 present the principal detailed-output screens.`

## Supplementary-material option
If the revised six-panel figure becomes unreadable at the journal's final column width, retain only four key panels in the main manuscript—personal input, work-area selection, machine/malfunction selection, and overall risk summary—and place the complete interface sequence in a supplementary figure:

`Supplementary Figure S1. Complete OSH-RA interface sequence from application entry and data input to the risk, precaution, legal-information, body-region, unmitigated-hazard, and cost-analysis modules.`

This is preferable to adding multiple low-resolution stand-alone figures to the main Results section.

## Image-quality and reporting requirements
- Use original high-resolution screenshots rather than enlarging the existing page raster.
- Crop screens consistently and use the same device scale and aspect ratio.
- Add panel labels `(a)`–`(f)` outside the screenshots.
- Ensure that text remains readable at the journal's final figure width.
- Use one interface language consistently or explain any unavoidable language differences.
- Remove or anonymize names, account identifiers, notifications, device identifiers, geolocation data, and any real personal information.
- Use synthetic case values only and state this in the caption or Methods section.
- Do not describe the screenshots as evidence of usability, validation, effectiveness, or user acceptance unless a formal usability study was conducted.

## Cross-comment consistency requirements
- Comment 12 controls whether any interface text may attribute scenario generation to an LLM.
- Comment 15 controls any displayed equipment-inventory counts.
- Comments 17 and 20 control interpretation of the displayed overall percentage.
- Comment 21 controls payment-day, CVaR, and cost terminology.
- Comment 22 controls the separate descriptions of Figures 16, 17, and 18.
- The legal-information screen must not be described as individualized legal advice.
