Spatial evidence:
- Code-Point Open assigns each postcode unit a National Grid reference point.
- The point is the nearest delivery point to the calculated mean position of delivery points in the postcode unit.
- Coordinates are supplied at 1 metre resolution.
- Great Britain coordinates use British National Grid / OSGB36, EPSG:27700.
- The exact sample above therefore provides source point WKT POINT(437292 115542) in EPSG:27700.
- Geometry is retained as explicit source-coordinate properties/WKT rather than inserted into GeoJSON geometry, to avoid silently treating BNG coordinates as RFC 7946 WGS84 coordinates.
