OFFICIAL PUBLIC NO-LOGIN SOURCE WINDOW — Ordnance Survey OS VectorMap Local sample data
Checked 2026-09-29 UTC.

Ordnance Survey describes OS VectorMap Local as a highly detailed, customisable street-level map showing fences,
building outlines, paths and street names. The official public sample-data page exposes sample areas for Exeter,
Inverness and Newport, with raster/vector structures and GML 3.2, GeoPackage and GeoTIFF supply formats.
The page reports version February 2025 and states that sample data are available under the Sample Data Licence.

This is a materially different source family from the preceding OS Open TOID generalized-location route because it
directly advertises building outlines. However, the available project evidence does not provide a deterministic
canonical_parcel_id binding from these sample building outlines, and this sample product description does not prove
a building type for a canonical parcel. The sample-area limitation also prevents treating it as a canonical national
parcel classification source. No record is accepted.
