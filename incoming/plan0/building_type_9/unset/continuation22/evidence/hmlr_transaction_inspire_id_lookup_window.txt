Evidence:
HMLR states that this lookup contains the primary identifiers from published Price Paid Information and
published INSPIRE Index Polygons and enables users to join those datasets to geolocate price-paid records.
The specification states that INSPIRE_ID is the same as POLY_ID published in HMLR's National Polygon Dataset.
Fields are Transaction unique identifier (38-character identifier) and INSPIRE ID (up to 9 digits).
The dataset contains identifiers only.
