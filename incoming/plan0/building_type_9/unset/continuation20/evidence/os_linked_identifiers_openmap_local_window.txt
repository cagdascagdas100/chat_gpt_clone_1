Evidence and material method change:
OS Open Linked Identifiers documents an authoritative BLPU (UPRN) <-> TopographicArea (TOID) relationship.
Its TopographicArea comes from OS MasterMap Topography Layer and is limited in this relationship family to
address/road features. OS states that this relationship can link address data with a building footprint.

OS OpenMap–Local is an open vector product. Its ImportantBuilding feature has polygon/surface geometry and
attributes including classification and BuildingTheme; the documented building themes include education,
medical care, religious buildings, retail, transport, sport/exercise and other listed categories.

This creates a materially new candidate chain:
UPRN -> authoritative TopographicArea TOID relation -> building geometry/classification source,
rather than continuation19's HMLR INSPIRE title/index polygon geometry route.

Acceptance gate:
The public window still does not expose this project's canonical_parcel_id inventory/polygons or an exact
canonical_parcel_id↔UPRN/TOID binding. No synthetic canonical identifier or geometry is permitted.
No project canonical parcel was therefore accepted or assigned a building type.
