Evidence:
The dataset contains HM Land Registry Price Paid transaction unique identifiers and UPRNs.
HMLR states UPRNs are unique identifiers for addressable locations and that using transaction identifiers
with UPRNs enables matching/merging with other sources. The technical specification says the lookup depends
on the relationship between a price-paid transaction, the registered title, and the UPRN supplied through
the National Polygon Service Title Number and UPRN Look Up Table. It also states that this dataset contains
identifiers only and that Title Numbers are not published in Price Paid Data.

Material method change:
This introduces an official transaction -> UPRN route, distinct from continuation20's OS UPRN -> TOID route.
Together these public identifier families could support cross-source binding where the project's canonical
parcel inventory exposes a compatible transaction, UPRN, title, TOID, or exact polygon relationship.
