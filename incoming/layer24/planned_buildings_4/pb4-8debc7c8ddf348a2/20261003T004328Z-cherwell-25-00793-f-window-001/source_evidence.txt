Official Cherwell District Council Planning Register application 25/00793/F. Application Type: Full Development. Status: Application Permitted. Valid date: 25/03/2025. Location: Hazel Hedge Cottage Tithe Lane Clifton Oxfordshire OX15 0PU. Proposal: Single and two storey extensions to dwelling; demolition of existing garage and erection of double garage with home office above, for ancillary use only. Decision: Permitted. Decision Issued Date: 17/07/2025. Official documents include Site Location Plan/Block Plan drawing 25001-P01-B; Proposed garage elevations/floor plans drawing 25001-P04-A; and Proposed elevations/floor plans drawing 25001-P03-E. The official Decision Notice condition 2 confirms approved drawings 25001-PB01B, 25001-P03E and 25001-P04A; condition 7 expressly identifies the permitted garage/office building and restricts it to ancillary or incidental residential use. The proposal therefore identifies one new distinct double-garage/home-office structure. Single and two storey extensions are works to the existing dwelling and the existing garage is demolished, so they are not counted as additional distinct new structures. Geometry/materialization is deferred to local OpenCode.
