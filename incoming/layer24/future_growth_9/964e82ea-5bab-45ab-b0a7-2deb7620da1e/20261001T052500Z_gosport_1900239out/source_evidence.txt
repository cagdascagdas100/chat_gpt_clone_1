Official Gosport Borough Council Regulatory Board agenda, 16 October 2019, Item 01, application 19/00239/OUT. The report states the hybrid application seeks full planning permission for erection of six mixed-use hangars and outline permission for one mixed-use building. It states the six proposed hangars are two-storey buildings with a 12.7m x 16.0m footprint and 8.3m height. This batch counts the six hangars only as distinct planned buildings; the additional outline application is not counted as a building because the source does not provide a distinct building count for it.
